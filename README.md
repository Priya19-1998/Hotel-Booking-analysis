# Hotel-Booking-analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes hotel booking data to identify booking patterns, cancellation trends, customer behavior, revenue-related insights, and factors that influence hotel bookings.

The project demonstrates practical **Data Analyst skills** including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, visualization, time series analysis, segmentation, and business insight generation.

## Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Clean and prepare the hotel booking dataset.
* Understand booking and cancellation patterns.
* Analyze customer and market segment behavior.
* Study revenue-related metrics such as ADR.
* Identify trends in hotel bookings over time.
* Analyze special requests and booking characteristics.
* Generate meaningful business insights from the data.

## Tools & Technologies

* Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

## Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning

* Checked the dataset structure and data types.
* Identified missing values.
* Handled missing and inconsistent data.
* Prepared the dataset for further analysis.

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

* Analyzed numerical and categorical variables.
* Examined booking-related statistics.
* Studied important measures such as average daily rate (ADR).

### 3. Booking & Cancellation Analysis

* Analyzed the overall booking and cancellation patterns.
* Compared cancellation behavior across different hotel types.
* Identified factors associated with higher cancellation rates.

### 4. Time Series Analysis

* Analyzed booking trends over time.
* Studied monthly and yearly booking patterns.
* Identified periods with higher and lower booking activity.

### 5. Customer & Market Segmentation

* Analyzed different market segments.
* Compared booking behavior across customer groups.
* Studied cancellation rates by market segment.

### 6. ADR Analysis

* Examined Average Daily Rate (ADR).
* Compared ADR across different booking characteristics.
* Analyzed ADR by distribution channel.

### 7. Special Requests Analysis

* Studied the number of special requests made by customers.
* Analyzed the relationship between special requests and booking behavior.

## Key Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* Major booking and cancellation patterns.
* Customer segments with different cancellation behaviors.
* Seasonal variations in hotel bookings.
* Differences in ADR across distribution channels.
* Customer behavior based on special requests.
* Factors that can potentially affect hotel revenue and occupancy.
